# Gloam: Design Rules
- Palette: pumpkin #ff7100, lantern #fd9702, glow #e102ff, dusk #ae03ff. Night gradient #4A1C7E to #2E0F55.
- Cards are frosted glass: white at 13%, 1.5px border white at 28%, backdrop blur.
- Type: Caprasimo for headings and big numbers, DM Sans for body text.
- Mobile first at 390 x 844. Provide a reduced-effects mode. Flicker never exceeds 3 flashes per second.
- Estimated streets must be distinguishable without colour alone.
